About FoundACrack

FoundACrack is an AI-powered platform dedicated to improving road safety and infrastructure maintenance.

Our mission is to make it easy for citizens to report road damage, and for authorities to receive and manage those reports efficiently.

Using state-of-the-art computer vision technology, we automatically detect and classify road damage, streamlining the entire reporting process.
